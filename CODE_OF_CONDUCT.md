# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

This is a small open-source project. Interactions here should be:

- Respectful and constructive
- Focused on the technical work
- Welcoming to contributors of all experience levels

Examples of acceptable behavior:
- Giving and receiving feedback graciously
- Acknowledging different approaches and tradeoffs
- Being direct about problems without being personal

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive comments
- Spam or off-topic promotion

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to **tides-mobile.human695@passmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed and addressed at the maintainer's discretion, which may include removing comments, closing issues, or blocking users.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication tied to this repository.
