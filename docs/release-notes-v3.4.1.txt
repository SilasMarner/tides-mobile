v3.4.1 (build 58) — Water Temp map, turbidity & upwelling alerts

NEW: Water Temp map — three NOAA satellite layers on the pannable map:
• Sea-surface temperature gradient (JPL MUR, 1 km daily) — find the breaks and edges where fish stack up.
• Upwelling — water temperature vs. normal; blue = colder-than-normal water moving in.
• Turbidity — water clarity at a glance (blue = clear, yellow/red = murky).

NEW: Upwelling advisory on the Conditions card plus an optional once-a-day alert when water near your station runs well colder than normal.

All maps now live under one globe menu on the station screen. User Guide updated.
